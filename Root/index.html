About WCode

WCode is an encoding schema where digital data is mapped to a sequence of simple, unique nouns.

Unlike barcodes, WCodes use literal words that can be spoken, handwritten, memorized, or typed with zero ambiguity.

Technology: Base-1024 encoding using 1024 unique nouns. Each word represents exactly 10 bits of data, with optional CRC-8 checksum verification.

For documentation, use-cases, and specifications, visit the About & Documentation page or check the 1024 WordList.
