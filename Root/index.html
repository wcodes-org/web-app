\ Word 1 Word 2 Word 3 /
e.g. \ apple banana cherry /

An encoding schema where digital data is mapped to a sequence of simple, unique nouns.

  • 1,024 Words: Base-1024 dictionary of common nouns. Each word encodes 10 bits of data.
  • Error Detection: Optional CRC-8 checksum verification protects against transcription mistakes.

Steps

  • Enter words or data to encode or decode between formats.
  • Choose a data type or let Auto detect it automatically.
  • Speak words, copy outputs, share, or save encoded codes to history.
